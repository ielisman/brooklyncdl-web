How to Get Your CDL in New York

A step-by-step guide from Brooklyn CDL Inc., a top CDL driving program serving Red Hook, Mill Basin, Flatbush, Bensonhurst, Brighton Beach, Sheepshead Bay, Gravesend, other Brooklyn areas and drivers from Queens, the Bronx, Staten Island, Manhattan, and Long Island.

Step by Step

How to Get a CDL License: The Complete Process

From Permit to Road Test

The 8-step process, in order

  1. 1
    Hold a valid New York State driver's license.
  2. 2
    Study the New York CDL manual and choose your class (A, B, or C) and any endorsements — click for details.
  3. 3
    Pass your DOT medical exam and obtain a medical certificate.
  4. 4
    Pass the CDL knowledge test at the DMV and receive your Commercial Learner Permit (CLP).
  5. 5
    Complete FMCSA-registered ELDT (Entry-Level Driver Training) — required before your road test.
  6. 6
    Take behind-the-wheel lessons with a licensed CDL school, covering pre-trip inspection, backing, and on-road driving.
  7. 7
    Pay the DMV skills test fee. We will schedule your road test
  8. 8
    Pass the road test — vehicle inspection, basic controls, and on-road driving — to receive your CDL.
Common Questions

CDL How-To: Frequently Asked Questions

What is CDL Class A, Class B, and Class C?

  1. Class A: combination vehicles over 26,001 lbs with a towed unit over 10,000 lbs (tractor-trailers).
  2. Class B: single vehicles over 26,001 lbs, or towing under 10,000 lbs (straight trucks, buses).
  3. Class C: smaller vehicles carrying 16+ passengers or placarded hazardous materials.

How do I study the New York CDL manual?

  1. Download the free New York State Commercial Driver's Manual from the DMV.
  2. Class A: read Chapters 2 & 3 (General Knowledge), Chapter 5 (Air Brakes), and Chapter 6 (Combination Vehicles).
  3. Class B: read Chapters 2, 3, and 5. Add Chapter 4 (Transporting Passengers) for the P endorsement, or Chapter 10 (School Buses) for the S endorsement.
  4. Class C: read Chapters 2 & 3.
  5. Practice with free NY CDL practice tests before your DMV knowledge test.

How do I get a CDL Commercial Learner's Permit (CLP)?

  1. Bring ID, proof of residency, and your driver's license to the DMV.
  2. Pass the CDL knowledge test for your class and endorsements.
  3. Pay the permit fee and hold the CLP at least 14 days before your road test.

What is ELDT and why do you need it?

  1. ELDT (Entry-Level Driver Training) is federally required for new CDL applicants and upgrades.
  2. It must be completed with an FMCSA-registered provider like Sea Star Commercial Driving School Inc.
  3. Your certification is submitted directly to the DMV so you can schedule your road test.

How do I pay the DMV road test scheduling fee?

Option 1 — You Have a MyDMV Profile

Log in at my.ny.gov, select MyDMV, and skip directly to Step 4 below.

Or jump to Step 2 if no license exists under your MyDMV profile.

Option 2 — Pay Without a MyDMV Profile

  1. 1
    Open the NY DMV Road Test Portal.
  2. 2
    Enter your Client ID (front of license), Document ID (back of license), Date of Birth, and ZIP Code, then click Submit.
    Tip
    • Document ID can be found under Doc # (front or back of the license) or letters coming after "IDUSA" (back of the license at the bottom).
    • If your current form isn't accepted, try the Document ID from your previous license.
    Still No Luck?If no forms are accepted, visit your local DMV office to pay the fee in person.
  3. 3
    Enter the last 4 digits of your SSN. Set 3 security questions (using the same simple answer for all three saves time) and confirm your contact information if prompted.
    NoteIf security questions were already set up before, you'll be asked to answer one of them instead of the SSN.
  4. 4
    Locate the Class A Commercial Learner Permit section and select View/Manage Road Tests.
  5. 5
    Under Additional Actions, click Purchase additional tests to complete your payment.
    NoteIf this link does not appear, your fee is already paid.

How do I obtain a Hazmat endorsement?

  1. Pass a TSA background check and fingerprinting.
  2. Pass the Hazmat knowledge test at the DMV.
  3. Renew the endorsement and background check every 5 years.

How do I prepare for and pass the CDL road test?

  1. Practice pre-trip vehicle inspection until it's memorized.
  2. Drill backing maneuvers and basic vehicle controls in a parking lot or range.
  3. Complete supervised on-road driving sessions with your instructor before test day.
