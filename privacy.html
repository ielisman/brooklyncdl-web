Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-07

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Brooklyn CDL ("we", "us") handles personal information in the CDL-Team platform, a web and mobile application used by commercial driver's license (CDL) training schools to manage students, instructors, scheduling, training records, payments and communications.

In most cases the driving school that you train with decides what information is entered about you. Brooklyn CDL operates the platform and processes that information on the school's behalf.

2. Information we collect

Account and contact details: name, e-mail address, phone number, mailing address and, where the school enters it, date of birth.

Training and licensing records: driver license, learner permit and medical card information, training and ELDT records, test results, and photos or documents that you or the school upload.

Activity records: class schedules, appointments and attendance, payments and receipts, messages and notes.

Technical data: device and push notification tokens, and basic technical logs such as browser type, IP address and error reports.

3. How we use information

We use information to run the school's operations: creating and managing accounts, scheduling lessons, keeping training and ELDT records, processing payments and issuing receipts, and sending reminders, confirmations and other notifications.

We also use technical data to keep the service secure, fix problems and improve reliability.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share information only with service providers that process it for us, and only as needed to run the service: Supabase (database hosting and authentication), Google (sign-in and Firebase push notifications), Twilio and Plivo (text messages), Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (file storage and backups) and our e-mail delivery provider.

Within the platform, information is visible to the school's authorized staff and to you. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

5. Text messages and push notifications

If you provide a phone number and agree to receive messages, the school may send you schedule confirmations, reminders and account notices by text message or push notification. Message frequency varies.

Reply STOP to a text message at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to messages is not a condition of purchasing any service.

6. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as your account or the school's account is active and as long as needed to meet legal, training-record, tax and accounting obligations. After that it is deleted or anonymized.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls limiting each school's data to that school, and regular backups. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private and tell us right away if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information by e-mailing info@brooklyncdl.com. We may need to verify your identity, and some records may have to be kept where the law requires it. You can also ask your school to update information about you.

9. Children

The platform is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy: Brooklyn CDL, info@brooklyncdl.com.
