Pricing Questions

CDL Training Cost — Frequently Asked Questions

How much does CDL training cost in Brooklyn?

  1. Single lessons run $110/hour (automatic) or $130/hour (manual).
  2. Full packages range from $1,600 to $3,380 depending on hours, transmission, and number of road tests.
  3. All packages include ELDT (Entry-Level Driver Training), which is required by the FMCSA before your road test.

What is included in a CDL training package?

  1. Behind-the-wheel lessons in the same truck you'll test in.
  2. ELDT theory and practical certification, submitted directly to the DMV.
  3. Road test scheduling support and the DMV skills fee.

Is there a lower-cost or free CDL training option?

  1. Brooklyn CDL Inc. partners with non-profit and workforce organizations that offer grant-funded CDL training.
  2. Eligible drivers can have some or all tuition covered.
  3. See our Free Training page or call/text us to confirm eligibility.

How do automatic and manual CDL training prices compare?

  1. Manual transmission training costs more per hour ($130 vs. $110) because it requires more practice time.
  2. Manual-licensed drivers can operate both automatic and manual trucks, which some employers prefer.
  3. Automatic-only CDLs restrict you to automatic-transmission vehicles.
