About Brooklyn CDL Inc.

A New York City based CDL training school helping drivers get licensed and get to work.

Who We Are

Brooklyn CDL Inc. is a Commercial Driver's License training school serving New York drivers who want to start or advance a career in trucking and commercial driving. We provide classroom instruction, hands-on behind-the-wheel training, and guidance through the DMV licensing and road test process.

Our instructors are experienced, licensed professionals who focus on building safe, confident drivers — not just passing a test. Whether you're pursuing a Class A tractor-trailer license or a Class B straight-truck license, we tailor training to your experience level and goals.

What We Focus On

  • Real behind-the-wheel practice, not just theory.
  • Clear guidance through DMV paperwork, fees, and scheduling.
  • Flexible scheduling for working students.
  • Honest advice on which CDL class fits your career goals.
Our Approach

Why Students Choose Us

Personalized Instruction

Small class sizes and one-on-one driving time mean instructors actually get to know your strengths and where you need practice.

Safety First

Career Focused

We help you understand what employers look for so your CDL opens doors, not just checks a box.
