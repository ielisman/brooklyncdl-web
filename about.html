About Brooklyn CDL Inc.

A CDL training program helping drivers across all Brooklyn areas including Red Hook, Flatbush, Bensonhurst, Brighton Beach, Sheepshead Bay, Mill Basin, Sheepshead Bay, Gravesend, as well as Queens, the Bronx, Staten Island, Manhattan, and Long Island get licensed and get to work. Who We Are

Brooklyn's CDL training program

Brooklyn CDL Inc. is a Commercial Driver's License training program serving New York drivers who want to start or advance a career in trucking and commercial driving. We provide top of the line education, hands-on behind-the-wheel training, and guidance through the DMV licensing and road test process.

Our instructors are experienced, licensed professionals who focus on building safe, confident drivers — not just passing a test. We tailor training to your experience level and goals.

What We Focus On

What sets our training apart

  • Real behind-the-wheel practice, not just theory.
  • Clear guidance through DMV paperwork, fees, and scheduling.
  • Honest advice on which CDL class fits your career goals.
